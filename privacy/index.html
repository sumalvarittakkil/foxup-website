Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 October 2026 · Last updated: 13 November 2026

1. About this policy

This policy explains what personal data the FoxUP Pay app collects and how it is used. The app runs on FoxUP payment terminals at retail shops in the UAE, where it sells mobile recharges, data packages, vouchers, international top-ups and utility bill payments.

The app is operated by FoxUP, Dubai, United Arab Emirates ("FoxUP", "we"). This policy covers customers who buy a recharge, voucher or top-up, or pay a bill, at a shop using the app.

2. Information the app collects

The app collects a mobile number or an account number for every sale. This number is the account ID that tells the operator or biller where to apply the recharge or payment.

DataWhen it is collectedWhat it is used for
Mobile numberRecharges, data packages, vouchers and international top-upsThe account ID of the line receiving the recharge or top-up
Account numberBill paymentsThe account ID the bill payment is credited to
Contact mobile number (optional)Bill payments, if the customer chooses to give oneContacting the customer about their payment
Transaction detailsEvery saleProduct, amount, fees, tip, date and time, shop, terminal and reference number

The app does not ask customers for their name, address, ID or email.

3. How the app uses this information

The app uses this information only to:

  1. Send the mobile or account number and the amount to the operator or biller, so the recharge or payment reaches the right account
  2. Print the receipt and keep a record of the sale for the retailer and for FoxUP
  3. Check failed or disputed transactions and issue reversals or refunds
  4. Detect fraud, duplicate transactions and misuse of terminals
  5. Meet accounting, tax and other legal record-keeping duties in the UAE

We do not sell this information, and we do not use customer mobile or account numbers for marketing.

4. Sharing and card payments

The mobile or account number is shared only with the parties needed to complete the sale:

  • Mobile operators, billers and top-up suppliers, to apply the recharge or payment to the right account. For international top-ups, this includes operators outside the UAE
  • Our hosting and IT providers, which store and process data on our behalf under contract
  • Geidea, our card payment partner, which processes card payments on the terminal (see below)
  • The retailer where the sale was made, who sees their own sales history
  • UAE authorities, when the law requires it

Card payments. The FoxUP app never sees or stores card numbers, expiry dates, security codes or PINs. Card payments are handled inside our payment partner Geidea's own secure application on the terminal, which returns only the payment result to the app.

What the app does not access. The app does not access the terminal's contacts, photos, microphone or camera.

5. Retention, security and your rights

Retention. Transaction records, including mobile and account numbers, are kept for 5 years to meet UAE accounting, tax and legal requirements, then deleted or anonymised.

Security. Data is sent over encrypted connections, stored on secured servers, and accessible only to staff who need it for their role.

Your rights. Under the UAE Personal Data Protection Law, you can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, or object to how it is used. Completed transactions cannot be deleted while the law requires us to keep them. To make a request, email privacy@foxuppay.com with the mobile or account number and roughly when and where the purchase was made.

Changes. We may update this policy and will post the latest version at foxuppay.com/privacy.

Contact. Email privacy@foxuppay.com.

6. This website

This section covers foxuppay.com itself.

Cookies and analytics. The website does not set cookies and does not use analytics or advertising trackers. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads. If we add analytics in future, we will update this section first.

Contact form. The "Become a FoxUP retailer" form asks for your shop name, emirate, mobile/WhatsApp number and preferred language. The website does not store these details: the form opens a WhatsApp message to our sales team, and nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp. We use what you send only to reply about becoming a FoxUP retailer. If you email sales@foxuppay.com instead, we use your email in the same way.
