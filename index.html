FAQ

Questions retailers ask us.

How much does the terminal cost?

Pricing depends on your shop and the current offer. Message us on WhatsApp and we'll share today's terminal price and availability.

Is there a deposit or a minimum wallet top-up?

We confirm any deposit and the minimum wallet top-up with you before you sign up, so there are no surprises. Ask us on WhatsApp for the current terms.

How much commission do I earn?

You earn commission on every sale. The rate depends on the service (local recharge, international top-up, bills or vouchers). We share the full rate card when you apply.

Can my customers pay cash or card?

Both. FoxUP takes cash and card on the same handheld, so every customer who walks in can buy.

How fast does my wallet update?

Commission is credited instantly when a sale is approved, and your balance updates live on the terminal.

What happens if the terminal stops working?

Call the support number on the sticker on your terminal, or message us on WhatsApp. Our local support team will help you get back up and running.

What do I need to apply?

Your Trade Licence, your Emirates ID, and a counter with power and internet.
