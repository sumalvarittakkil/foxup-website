The product

One terminal. Every recharge, bill and payment.

A closer look at how foxup turns a single compact device into a full service counter for your customers.

01. Merchant Wallet

Your balance, always live, always yours.

Every terminal is tied to a merchant wallet. Load it by card on the device itself or top up manually with cash, either way, your balance updates immediately and is ready to spend on stock, bills or a cash out.

  • Top up via terminal or manual cash load
  • Real time balance, credit and commission view
  • No separate settlement account to manage
